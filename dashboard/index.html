The collective

Not a wrapper: a loop of agents around NVIDIA ALCHEMI

ALCHEMI is the engine at the centre. Around it the agents decide what is worth running, try to prove every answer wrong, and send work round again when it fails, until the trust rule lets a result count. People and the chemistry lead set the rules before any of it ran.
